Privacy Policy

Application: Phone2Pc • Effective Date: October 13, 2026

Welcome to Phone2Pc ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Li Jane. We value your trust and are strictly committed to safeguarding your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of information handled by Phone2Pc, the purposes for which data is used, and how your privacy is protected.

1. Architecture & Local Data Privacy

Phone2Pc is designed to connect your mobile device and computer directly or through your personal cloud accounts. All transfer operations, clipboard synchronization, and temporary transmission caches remain entirely under your control. We do not host proprietary central relay servers that inspect, monitor, store, sell, or monetize your transmitted content.

2. Google Drive Integration & User Data Protection

Phone2Pc provides an optional cloud relay and backup feature utilizing the Google Drive API. When you link your Google Account to enable cloud sync between your phone and PC, the following strict guidelines apply:

🛡 Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Phone2Pc's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Scope of Access: We strictly request the dedicated application permission (drive.file scope). Phone2Pc only creates, reads, and updates files and backup archives that were created directly by Phone2Pc itself. The App does not have permission to view, read, modify, or delete any other personal files, folders, or photos in your Google Drive.
  • Purpose of Access: Google Drive integration is used strictly to upload files, backup data, or synchronize clipboard packages between your authorized devices at your explicit direction.
  • No Third-Party Transmission: Your Google authentication tokens, profile details, and synchronized file contents are never transmitted to third parties, advertising networks, or analytics servers. Communication occurs directly between your device and Google's official Drive endpoints over secure HTTPS.
  • No AI Model Training: None of your Google user data or synchronized files are ever used to train, develop, or improve generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

3. Device Permissions Requested

  • Storage / Media: Required to select files, documents, and photos on your device for transfer to your PC or for cloud backup.
  • Network / Internet: Required to establish local Wi-Fi transfers with your PC and to communicate securely with Google Drive API endpoints.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used to notify you of file transfer progress, successful completions, or transfer errors in the background.

4. Data Retention, Management & Revocation

  • You maintain full ownership and control over all transmitted files. Transferred files are saved directly to your destination folders and can be deleted at any time.
  • Any cloud sync files placed in your Google Drive can be managed or deleted directly through the Google Drive interface.
  • You may disconnect and immediately revoke Phone2Pc's access to your Google Account at any moment via your Google Account Permissions Settings.
