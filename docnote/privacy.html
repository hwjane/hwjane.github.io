Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

Welcome to DocNote ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Li Jane. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your data.

1. Data Storage & Offline-First Principle

DocNote is designed as an offline-first application. All your notes, tags, attachments, and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate proprietary cloud servers to collect, upload, or monetize your note contents.

2. Google Drive Integration & User Data

The App provides an optional cloud backup and restore feature using the Google Drive API. When you choose to use this feature, the App interacts with Google Drive subject to the following rules:

🛡 Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

DocNote's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Scope of Access: We request only the permissions necessary to create, read, and update backup zip archives explicitly generated by DocNote (using dedicated application storage / drive.file scope). We do not read, modify, or access any unrelated files in your Google Drive.
  • Purpose of Access: The access is strictly used to create archive backups of your notes and restore them onto your device upon your request.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your Google account information, credentials, and backup files are never transmitted to any third-party servers, advertisers, or external services. All transfers occur directly between your device and Google's official Drive servers via HTTPS.
  • No AI Model Training: Your Google user data is never used to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

3. Device Permissions Used

  • Storage / Photos / Media: Required solely to read documents, save exported notes, and attach files as specified by the user.
  • Camera: Optional; used only when you choose to capture documents or photos for OCR text extraction.
  • Internet: Used exclusively for Google Drive backup/restore synchronization and standard ad network services (if applicable).

4. Data Retention and Revocation

You have complete control over your data:

  • You can delete your local notes and backup files from within the app at any time.
  • You can disconnect and revoke DocNote's access to your Google Drive account anytime through your Google Account Security Settings.
